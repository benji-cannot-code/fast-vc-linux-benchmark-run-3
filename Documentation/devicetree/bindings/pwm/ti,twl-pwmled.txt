Texas Instruments TWL series PWM drivers connected to LED terminals

Supported PWMs:
On TWL4030 series: PWMA and PWMB (connected to LEDA and LEDB terminals)
On TWL6030 series: LED PWM (mainly used as charging indicator LED)
