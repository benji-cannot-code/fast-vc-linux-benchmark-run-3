Bindings for Synaptics AS370 PVT sensors

Required properties:
- compatible : "syna,as370-hwmon"
- reg        : address and length of the register set.

Example:
	hwmon@ea0810 {
		compatible = "syna,as370-hwmon";
		reg = <0xea0810 0xc>;
	};
