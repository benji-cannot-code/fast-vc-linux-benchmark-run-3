Bindings for MAX1619 Temperature Sensor

Required properties:
- compatible : "maxim,max1619"
- reg        : I2C address, one of 0x18, 0x19, 0x1a, 0x29, 0x2a, 0x2b, 0x4c, or
               0x4d, 0x4e

Example:
	temp@4c {
		compatible = "maxim,max1619";
		reg = <0x4c>;
	};
