NAU8822 audio CODEC

This device supports I2C only.

Required properties:

  - compatible : "nuvoton,nau8822"

  - reg : the I2C address of the device.

Example:

codec: nau8822@1a {
	compatible = "nuvoton,nau8822";
	reg = <0x1a>;
};
