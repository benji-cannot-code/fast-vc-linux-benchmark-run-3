	- clock-names:	Must one of  the following "mclk1", "xtal", "mclk2"

	- clocks:	phandle of the clock that provides the codec sysclk

Example:

	compatible = "tempo,tscs42A2";
	reg = <0x69>;
	clock-names = "xtal";
	clocks = <&audio_xtal>;
