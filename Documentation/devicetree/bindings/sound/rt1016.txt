RT1016 Stereo Class D Audio Amplifier

This device supports I2C only.

Required properties:

- compatible : "realtek,rt1016".

- reg : The I2C address of the device.


Example:

rt1016: codec@1a {
	compatible = "realtek,rt1016";
	reg = <0x1a>;
};
