Device-Tree bindings for Digital microphone (DMIC) codec

This device support generic PDM digital microphone.

Required properties:
	- compatible: should be "dmic-codec".

Example node:

	dmic_codec: dmic@0 {
		compatible = "dmic-codec";
	};
