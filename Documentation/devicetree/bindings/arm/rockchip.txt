- bq Curie 2 tablet:
    Required root node properties:
      - compatible = "mundoreader,bq-curie2", "rockchip,rk3066a";

- Radxa Rock board:
    Required root node properties:
      - compatible = "radxa,rock", "rockchip,rk3188";
