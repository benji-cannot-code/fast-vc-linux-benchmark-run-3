Binding for Fairchild FAN53555 regulators

Required properties:
  - compatible: one of "fcs,fan53555", "fcs,fan53526", "silergy,syr827" or
		"silergy,syr828"
  - reg: I2C address

Optional properties:
  - fcs,suspend-voltage-selector: declare which of the two available
